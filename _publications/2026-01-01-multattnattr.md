This paper introduces MultAttnAttrib, a training-free attribution method that uses selected multimodal LLM attention heads to locate text and image evidence supporting answers in long documents. It also introduces MultAttrEval, a complementary benchmark for fine-grained multimodal attribution. The method outperforms strong prompting, captioning, and RAG baselines while running at substantially lower latency.

**Status:** Preprint available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01420); currently under review at ACL 2026.

**Key Contributions:**
- Training-free multimodal attribution via selected MLLM attention heads
- MultAttrEval benchmark for fine-grained multimodal attribution in long documents
- Strong accuracy with ~7× lower latency versus prompting-based baselines

**Applications:** Grounded document QA, AI assistants, enterprise multimodal RAG, and model interpretability.
